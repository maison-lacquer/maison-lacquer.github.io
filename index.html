      FAQ

      Good questions

      Can’t find your answer? Call us on 020 7946 0321 during opening hours.

      How long does gel last?

      Three to four weeks on hands and four to six on toes. After that the regrowth shows and lifting becomes more likely, so we suggest booking your next appointment at the three-week mark.

      How do you sterilise your tools?

      Metal tools are cleaned and sterilised between every client. Files, buffers and pedicure pads are single-use, so they’re never shared.

      Can I come in wearing gel or acrylics from another salon?

      Yes. Mention it in the notes when you book, so we set aside enough time to take it off first.

      How do I cancel or reschedule?

      Call us at least 24 hours before your appointment and we’ll find another time. That way someone else can take your slot.

      What’s the difference between gel, dip powder and acrylic?

      Gel is a polish cured under a lamp and suits natural nails. Dip powder (SNS) builds a stronger layer without a lamp and lasts a little longer. Acrylic is the strongest option and is used to add length or shape.

      What if I’m running late?

      Up to 15 minutes late and we can still do everything. Any later and we may need to simplify the design so the next client isn’t kept waiting.
